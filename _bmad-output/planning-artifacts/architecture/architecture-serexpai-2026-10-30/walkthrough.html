MBSC v1: architecture walkthrough

A guided tour of the spine. Normative Kernel + Encoder Shell. Source of truth: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md and bitstream-spec-seed.md.

The decoder is a small normative kernel. Everything that searches, tunes or reports sits outside it and depends on it, never the reverse. Click a crate.

Select a crate.

Every Candidate goes through one kernel encode_block, so a Baseline and a Formula differ only in the Predictor (AD-2). Click a step.

A Block is a header, parameters and its own coder stream (AD-8). Pick a kind to see the layout and its fixed cost.

Block cost is exact and additive (AD-8), so a dynamic program over the 256-byte grid gives the optimal partition (AD-9). This toy uses a made-up compressibility profile. Change the per-Block overhead and watch the partition move.
